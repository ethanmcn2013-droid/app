Unblock Project Drive

The floor is built and proved. The next package needs a Google account that only you can create, and two secrets that should never pass through a chat window. Eleven steps, in three sittings. Then I take it back.

You need your Google account You need a second Google account Cost nothing

Before you start

  • ✓ Twenty-five minutes, or three shorter sittings. The guide is split into three parts and you can stop between them. Nothing half-finished breaks anything.
  • ✓ A second Google account. Any spare Gmail. The whole promise of this feature is that a person you invite can open one folder and nothing else in your Drive — and there is no way to prove that with one account.
  • ✓ No card, no billing. Everything here is free. If a screen ever asks you to enable billing, you are on the wrong page — close it and re-read the step.
  • ✓ Nothing to paste back to me. Every secret goes straight into Vercel, where I already read the storage token from. Please do not paste a password or a client secret into our chat — I will ask you to rotate it if you do.

Part two

Set up Google

18 minutes
  1. 3

    Make a Google Cloud project

    A "project" here is just a container for the permission Signal Studio will ask people for. Go to console.cloud.google.com and sign in with the Google account you want to own this — use the one you run the business from, not a personal spare.

    Click The project name at the top › New project

    Name it Signal Studio. Leave the organisation as whatever it offers. Press Create and wait about ten seconds.

    You should see
    Signal Studio    in the project selector at the top of the page
    If it goes wrong

    If it asks for a billing account, you have landed on a paid product page. Back out. Nothing in this guide costs money — the Drive API has a free quota far beyond anything we will use.

  2. 4

    Turn on Google Drive

    Every Google service is off by default. This switches on the one we need. Make sure Signal Studio is the selected project at the top before you do this.

    Click ☰ menu › APIs & Services › Library › search "Google Drive API"

    Open the result called Google Drive API and press Enable.

    You should see
    API enabled    and the button changes to "Manage"
  3. 5

    Write the screen people will read

    When someone connects their Drive, Google shows them a screen asking whether they trust us. This step is where you write what that screen says. It is the most customer-facing thing in this whole guide.

    Click ☰ menu › Google Auth Platform › Branding
    • App name: Signal Studio — this is the name in the sentence "Signal Studio wants access to your Google Account".
    • User support email: your own address.
    • App home page: https://signalstudio.ie
    • Privacy policy: https://signalstudio.ie/privacy
    • Terms of service: https://signalstudio.ie/terms
    • Authorised domain: signalstudio.ie
    • Developer contact: your own address.

    Save.

    If it goes wrong

    Do not upload a logo. There is a slot for one and it is tempting. Adding a logo puts the whole app into Google's brand review queue, which takes days and blocks nothing else we need. We can add it any time later.

  4. 6

    Publish it now, not later

    Google starts every app in "Testing". This step moves it out. Do it today, before anything is built on top of it.

    Click ☰ menu › Google Auth Platform › Audience › Publish app

    The audience type should read External. Press Publish app and confirm.

    You should see
    Publishing status: In production
    Why this one matters more than it looks

    While the status says Testing, Google silently throws away every connection after seven days and caps you at 100 users. Customers would connect their Drive, it would work all week, and the following Monday it would be broken — with nothing in our logs to explain it. It looks exactly like a bug in our own code, and we would spend a week hunting one that does not exist.

    If it goes wrong

    If Google shows a panel about verification, read it but do not be alarmed. We ask for one narrow permission that Google classifies as non-sensitive, so there is no security assessment and no audit. Verification here only governs whether our name and logo appear polished on the consent screen. If it will not publish without a privacy policy and terms link, those are the two URLs in step 5 and both are live.

  5. 7

    Ask for exactly one permission

    This is the security decision of the whole feature, and it is one checkbox. We ask only for the permission to touch files our own app created. The rest of your customer's Drive is not "data we promise not to read" — it is data our key cannot reach at all.

    Click ☰ menu › Google Auth Platform › Data Access › Add or remove scopes

    A filter box appears. Paste this into it, tick the single result, then press Update and Save.

    Copy this
    https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file
    You should see
    Your non-sensitive scopes    1
.../auth/drive.file    See, edit, create and delete only the specific
                       Google Drive files you use with this app
    If it goes wrong

    If anything appears under Your sensitive scopes or restricted scopes, you have picked the wrong one. Remove it. The ones to avoid are called drive, drive.readonly and drive.metadata — each of those reads the customer's entire Drive, and each triggers an annual third-party security audit.

  6. 8

    Create the two values I need

    These are the app's own username and password for talking to Google.

    Click ☰ menu › Google Auth Platform › Clients › Create client
    • Application type: Web application
    • Name: Signal Studio app

    Then find Authorised redirect URIs and press Add URI twice, pasting one of these into each. These are the two addresses Google is allowed to send a person back to after they say yes. Anything not on this list is refused, which is what stops somebody redirecting your customers somewhere else.

    Copy this — first URI
    https://app.signalstudio.ie/api/connections/google/callback
    Copy this — second URI
    http://localhost:3000/api/connections/google/callback

    Press Create.

    You should see
    A panel headed "OAuth client created", holding
  Client ID       ...apps.googleusercontent.com
  Client secret   GOCSPX-...
    Do not close that panel yet

    The client secret is shown once and never again. Press Download JSON and keep the file somewhere safe, or leave the panel open until you have finished the next step. If you lose it you can make a new secret, but the old one has to be deleted or it stays valid.

    If it goes wrong

    If the second URI is refused with "Invalid Redirect: must use a secure scheme", check you typed http and not https for the localhost one. Google allows plain http only for localhost, and it is strict about it.

  7. 9

    Put both into Vercel

    Not into our chat. Vercel is where the storage token already lives and where I read it from, so a secret never has to sit in a message history that gets scrolled back through.

    Open vercel.com › app › Settings › Environment Variables

    Add two. For each one, tick all three environments — Production, Preview and Development — so it works everywhere including on my machine.

    NameValueMark sensitive
    GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_ID the long one ending .apps.googleusercontent.com no need
    GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_SECRET the one starting GOCSPX- yes
    If it goes wrong

    Spelling matters exactly — the names above are what the code will look for. A typo here surfaces much later as "invalid_client" from Google, which reads like the credentials are wrong when in fact they were never found.

Part three

One key, one spare account

5 minutes
  1. 10

    Make the encryption key

    When someone connects their Drive, Google gives us a long-lived key to their account. That has to be encrypted before it touches the database. This is the secret that encrypts it — and there is no encryption anywhere in this codebase today, which is exactly why it is being done properly rather than copied from the one place that got it wrong.

    Press the Windows key, type PowerShell, open it, and run this. It prints one line of gibberish. That is the key.

    Type this
    node -e "console.log(require('crypto').randomBytes(32).toString('base64'))"
    You should see
    7QpX2vK... 44 characters, ending in =

    Add it to Vercel exactly as in step 9 — all three environments, marked sensitive.

    NameValueMark sensitive
    PROVIDER_TOKEN_KEY the line PowerShell just printed yes
    Generate it once, then leave it alone

    Everything encrypted with this key can only be read with this key. If it is ever changed, every customer who has connected their Drive has to reconnect it. That is survivable but visible, so it is worth not doing by accident.

  2. 11

    Have a second Google account to hand

    The claim this feature makes is that a person you invite to a board can open that board's folder and nothing else in your Drive. There is no way to test that with one account — you would only ever be checking that you can see your own files.

    Any second Gmail will do; a family one is fine. All I need from you is the email address, which is not a secret, so you can just tell me it in chat.

    If it goes wrong

    If the second account is on a Google Workspace domain rather than a plain Gmail, its administrator may block third-party apps entirely. A personal @gmail.com avoids that and is a truer test of what our customers will actually have.

Honesty about the handover

There is one click I am not allowed to make

Once I have the credentials I can build and run everything else. But at one point a Google screen will appear that says Signal Studio wants access to your Google Account, and someone has to press Allow. That is a person signing into their own account, and I do not sign into accounts or handle passwords — not because it would be hard, but because it is the line that keeps this safe.

So the shape of it is: you finish this guide, I build the connection, I send you a link, you press Allow twice — once as yourself, once as the second account — and I carry on from there. Expect two short interruptions, not a session of clicking.
