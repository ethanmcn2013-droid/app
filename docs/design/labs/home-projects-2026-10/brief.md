## Target
Three materially different Home and Projects directions mounted in the actual isolated Next App, with its real shell, fonts and tokens. Home helps a person identify the next useful action; Projects helps them understand and enter their work. This engagement ends at Ethan's design choice. It does not release a redesign.

Production credentials, migrations, data and deployment. Source route defaults remain byte-equivalent in behaviour when preview gates are absent.
