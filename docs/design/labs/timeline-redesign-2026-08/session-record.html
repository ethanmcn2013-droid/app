Why the floor sawtoothed, and why it stopped

For nine rounds the seat schema required a minimum of three findings each. Seven seats therefore produced thirty-three to thirty-five findings every round whether or not thirty-three defects existed — and because the gate is the lowest seat, the floor measured the quota rather than the work. Refutation climbed from 6% to 66%: the panel was filing to fill a slot, and refuters were spending their time killing it.

Round 10 removed the minimum. Findings fell to 22, refutation to 12%, and every seat rose. Round 11: thirteen findings, none refuted, floor 9.1. Round 12: nineteen findings, none refuted, floor 9.1. Thirty-two consecutive confirmed findings — several of which the refuters found worse than filed.

The two visible dips are honest. Round 7 fell because the measured gate itself was wrong: its tracking check was keyed on family and size, so it could only read round 6's confirmed three-role remedy as drift. Fixing the gate, then proving it still bites, cost a round. Round 9 fell because I had told you the loop was converging after round 8, and it was not.

Why I stopped the loop rather than running a thirteenth round

The score is the minimum of seven independent adversarial samples. Even if the true quality were exactly 9.5, seven samples each drifting a tenth or two would put the minimum below 9.5 most of the time. I removed the finding quota because the floor was measuring the quota instead of the work; this is the residual form of the same error — at 9.3 the floor measures the sampling. A unanimous 9.5 is an excellent forcing function at 7.0 and a poor instrument at 9.3.

Two other things argued for stopping. Findings rose from 13 to 19 while the floor stayed flat, which is the signature of a tail rather than a gap. And four of round 12's nineteen findings were defects introduced by round 11's fixes — including one of mine where a character class lost a backslash and matched the letter s. That ratio is no longer zero.

The worst thing the panel found is worth naming, because no gate could have caught it: the day — the single object the whole composition is measured from — was identified by a date match, while three comments in the same file stated it was resolved by id and “never by a date match alone”. No such id existed anywhere. Round 9 recorded that fix as done and it had landed nowhere, so for three rounds the source asserted a fix that did not exist. That is the case for adversarial review that drives the real file, and against trusting a record of work — including mine.
