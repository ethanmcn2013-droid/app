FINDING id=editor-opens-on-an-empty-88px-band
Seat: ui
Element: .b-edit padding-top and its absolutely-positioned .b-undo
Problem: The panel reserves 88px at its top for the reversibility bar, but that bar is visibility:hidden until something has been undone, so in the resting owner-editing state the panel opens with an 89px band of nothing above 'WHAT IT IS' against 16px on the other three sides — computed padding is 88px / 16px / 16px / 16px, panel top at y=597, first ink at y=686. It reads as a mistake, not as a reserve. It also costs the panel its foot: at the flagship 1440x960 that same 88px puts .b-editActs ('Done') at y=1034 and 'Delete this moment' at y=1085, both below the 960px fold, so the owner opens an editor whose commit control is off screen and nothing says so. Reserving the band is right; reserving it at the top, where the panel makes its first impression, and spending it on silence, is not.
Proposed fix: Move the reserve to the foot, where the bar already lives at narrow widths. Render .b-undo as the last child of .b-edit rather than the first, set .b-edit padding to 16px on all four sides plus padding-bottom: 88px, and change the absolute offsets on .b-edit .b-undo from top: 16px to bottom: 16px. The panel then opens on its first label, the bar still sits inside the panel and still never stands on the count, and Done and Delete rise 72px — back above the 960px fold at 1440.
