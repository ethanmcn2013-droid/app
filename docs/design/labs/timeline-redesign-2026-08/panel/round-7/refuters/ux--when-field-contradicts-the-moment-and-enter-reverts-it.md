FINDING id=when-field-contradicts-the-moment-and-enter-reverts-it
Seat: ux
Element: state=owner-editing, the edit panel's WHEN text field and the −7/−1/+1/+7 steppers
Problem: The steppers sit directly under the field they appear to drive, but they never write back to it. Press −1 twice on "Send the invitations" and the row moves to Thu 6 Aug, the readout says "Thursday 6 August 2026 · in 21 days", the measure re-lays — and the field the owner is looking at still reads "8 August 2026". Two facts, one moment, in the same 300px panel. Worse, the field is now armed: focus it and press Enter without typing a character and the moment jumps back to 8 August and the two presses are gone. It is announced as "moved 1 day later", which is true of the data and false about what the person did. Every other derived fact on this surface follows the move; the one control labelled with the date does not. Nothing in the 539 assertions covers the input's own value, because they all check the row, the readout and the accessible name.
Proposed fix: In the stepper handler, after committing the new date, set the WHEN input's value from the same F.fmt formatter that seeded it (skipping the write only while that input holds focus and the user has typed). In the field's Enter/commit handler, run F.parseDay on the value and return without touching the model, the history or the live region if the parsed ISO equals the moment's current date — a confirm on an unchanged field must be a no-op, not an edit.
