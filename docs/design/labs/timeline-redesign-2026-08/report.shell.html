    How the panel works
    
    
    
      Seven seats run in parallel with no knowledge of one another: UI composition,
      typography, interaction and states, UX and information design, brand and copy,
      product taste, and one seat that distrusts opinion and reads the source. Every
      finding a seat raises is then handed to a fresh agent whose only job is to
      refute it, and which defaults to refused when uncertain.
      
    
    
    
    
      The measured seat is automated and runs on every change: palette lock, type
      weights, families, WCAG AA contrast against the real composited backdrop, hit
      targets including pseudo-element expanders, the radii ladder and the motion
      tokens, across every state. It has passed every check since the palette was
      locked, which is why the panel's findings are about things automation cannot
      see.
