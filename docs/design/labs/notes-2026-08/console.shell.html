Drive it, do not look at it.

This is the real file, not a picture of it. Every control on the right writes the same attribute the product itself reads, and the three rooms are nothing but three preset combinations of five named design decisions. Nothing in this console can reach a fourth colour, because the colours in it are the master's own — the receipt below is generated from the master's stylesheet at build time, not typed by hand.

Locked

Type in the sheet and press Ctrl+Enter. Ctrl+Z reverses anything. Tab into the pile and walk it with the arrows; Enter opens a note and Esc puts it back. / searches. In the hand, T makes a task and K keeps it.

The three rooms

Every combination of the five decisions is buildable and on palette. There are 72 of them and none can reach a colour that is not Ink, Indigo or White.

The palette receipt

Generated from the master's own stylesheet at build time

Every colour token the file declares, resolved to which of the three it is and at what alpha. The build fails if any token is not Ink, Indigo or White, so this list cannot drift from what the product paints. Status in this product is carried by ink density and fill — a filled dot for a note waiting on you, an outlined chip for one that has crossed into Tasks, filled ink for the single destructive act — and never by hue.
