5 hues on screenNot on the palette lock

Cool zinc #3f3f46 as the type colour on 283 nodes, green #1b873f, red #ef4444, amber, indigo. The greys are the worse half: #3f3f46 is not a tint of #111111, it is a cool zinc, and it is the colour of nearly all the type.

12× font-weight: 500The dominant weight is a forbidden one

Declared twelve times in the Notes stylesheet against six for 600 and one for 400. The most-used weight in the product is the one weight the locked pairing does not permit.

939px discarded at 1920The screen is thrown away

1440 → notebook 945px. 1920 → 981px. Between them the product gains 480px of screen and spends 36px of it. Nearly half the window is undifferentiated white.

2 leading edgesThe header does not belong to its content

The header rule runs the full 1380px. Everything under it is inset 217px each side. The tabs sit 217px to the left of the composer's own left edge.

And the one that is not about pixels: the offline state — the product working exactly as designed, holding your words safely on the device — states its reassurance in red. The behaviour says nothing was lost. The colour says something broke.
