Confirmed and unfixed on the surface

one item

Non-text contrast on the twelve-week chart · defect · raised round 2

The eleven context columns composite to 1.48:1 against the card on paper and 1.70:1 on the dark twin. WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 for a graphical object carrying meaning, and these carry the whole shape of the reading.

Why it is still open. The obvious fix inverts the chart. The current week is a hatch that composites to 1.71:1 — fainter than the columns it would be contrasted against — and .bar.live paints in zero states, so darkening the context alone makes the live mark the weakest thing in the frame and the one the reader is meant to read first. The strip half of the same finding was fixed: marks inside the fortnight became a 2px ring, solid past it, which clears the floor on a fill difference rather than on hue.

Size: half a day to a day, and it is a design decision before it is an edit — the plate, the hatch and the live mark have to be re-derived together, then re-measured on both grounds. Measurements are in panel/round-2/refuters/chart-and-strip-marks-fall-under-the-non-text-floor.json.

The trap for whoever ports this: audit.mjs grades text contrast against the real composited backdrop and does not grade non-text contrast at all. A green measured gate is not a 1.4.11 pass and must not be read as one.

The largest open item is not a pixel

Nobody owns the port into src/. This skill owns the lab master, both gates, the ledger, the record and the five documents. It hands over a port packet; a separate build session lands it, with the product’s own tests and its own release gate.

Three prior engagements ran forty-five rounds, raised 1,358 findings, made 919 fixes and published fifteen artifacts — and shipped zero pixels. None of the three retrospectives raised it. Each ended with a lab master nobody had agreed to port, and the question of whose job that was stayed open by default rather than by decision.

Said plainly, because it should be heard before round one rather than after round twelve: a lab master at the bar is not a shipped product, and an engagement that ends here has not improved anything a customer can see.
