## A · {Name}

**Thesis.** One paragraph: the single argument this direction makes about
the product. If two directions share a thesis, they are one direction.

**What it deliberately sacrifices.**
- Honest costs, named. A direction with no sacrifices listed has not been
  thought through — every real direction gives something up.

**Where it breaks a standing decision.**
1. Name the decision, then make the argument. Standing decisions may be
   broken only in writing, here.

**Proposed token changes (additive, named).** `--x-…` values with hexes.
Nothing existing is touched during exploration.

**Candidate delight moments.** Recorded here; promoted to the operator's
delight process only at lock-in.
