02 · since the lock

What actually changed

Two things were named as not good enough. Neither was adjusted; both were rebuilt.

The palette

No grey, no white canvas. Every room is built from a named material, and the ink is tuned to the room rather than left neutral, so nothing on screen reads as a default.

The card, rebuilt

  • One optical baseline. The completion control lives in a fixed gutter, the title starts at a fixed x, and every line under it aligns to that same x. Nothing below the title can push the title around.
  • A date column. The time fact right-aligns on the title's own line, so scanning a tray gives a column of dates instead of a ragged pile.
  • Metadata stops being chip soup. One quiet line, at most one tag, always in the same order.
  • The assignee disappears when everyone on the visible board is the same person, which is most of the time for one operator.
  • Colour only where time matters. Red is overdue and says how late. Today is warm, not red, because nothing has gone wrong yet. The milestone is the one indigo.
  • Hover never changes height. The card lifts and the menu appears in space the card already had.
  • Selection is a ring in the accent, so it can never be confused with hover, and keyboard focus is a real ring rather than a colour change.
  • Done is never struck through. The work happened, it was not a mistake, and the title stays readable.
  • A long title takes two lines and the note clamps under it, so a card can never run away down the tray.
  • In flight the card tilts and lifts, carrying the only heavy shadow in the product.

How this was made

    

      One master file, docs/design/labs/tasks-2026-08/floor.html, serves all
      three rooms from one architecture, so what differs between them is only the material
      and the card anatomy. Content is the real review-mode fixture: The Orchard, events;
      Mara and Finn; the pinned sixteenth of July. Type is the real Geist. The vendored
      design-system tokens are untouched, and every colour here is a named additive
      extension.
    

    

      Exploration only. Local branch design/tasks-exploration, no pull request,
      no deploy. The chrome and navigation contract gates are knowingly unmet and get
      rewritten at implementation, once Tasks, Notes and Timeline are all decided.
