Taking a locked design to a gate

The direction is settled and is not being redesigned. This is the record of the elevation: the palette locked to three colours, then round after round of an independent seven-seat panel scoring the work against the standard of an award-winning studio that iterated on it for months. Every finding is put to a fresh agent whose job is to refute it, so only what survives gets fixed.

How the panel works

Seven seats run in parallel with no knowledge of one another: UI composition, typography, interaction and states, UX and information design, brand and copy, product taste, and one seat that distrusts opinion and reads the source. Every finding a seat raises is then handed to a fresh agent whose only job is to refute it, and which defaults to refused when uncertain.

The measured seat is automated and runs on every change: palette lock, type weights, families, WCAG AA contrast against the real composited backdrop, hit targets including pseudo-element expanders, the radii ladder and the motion tokens, across five states. It has passed every check since the palette was locked, which is why the panel's findings are about things automation cannot see.
