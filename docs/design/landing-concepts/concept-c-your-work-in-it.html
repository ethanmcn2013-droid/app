How it fits together

One line, from your head to everyone's page.

Notes is where it starts. Tasks is where it gets done. Timeline is what everyone else sees. Nothing to set up in between.

Timeline, shared

Send one link. They see where it stands.

Any timeline can go out as a public link. They open it on their phone and see what's done, what's next and how long is left.

  • No account needed. No app to download and nothing to sign up for. They tap the link and read.
  • Fewer "any news?" messages. The answer is already on the page they have.
  • Your working stays yours. They see the timeline. Never your notes, tasks or chat.

What it won't ask of you

Clarity, not configuration.

Made for wedding venues, event planners, trades, small agencies, freelancers, teachers and students. People with real deadlines who never wanted to become a project manager.

No setup

Open it and write your first line. That is the whole start. No templates to pick, no boards to build.

No jargon

To do, in progress, done. Late, today, next week. The words you would say to the person beside you.

Nothing to configure

No fields to design and no rules to write. It comes ready for real work on the first morning.

A briefing, not a dashboard

Each morning, Home says what needs you today: what's due, what's late, what's waiting on you. Then it gets out of the way.

A note from the founder

Project management without the project‑manager energy.

I'm a project manager by training. PMP, Lean Six Sigma Black Belt, the lot. I've watched good people give up on project software because it asked them to learn a new language first.

Signal Studio keeps the discipline that makes projects land and drops the jargon, the setup and the translation. You write down what's in your head. It becomes jobs with a person and a day. Everyone else sees one page that says where things stand.

That's the whole idea. I'd love you to try it in January.

The founderSignal Studio, Ireland
