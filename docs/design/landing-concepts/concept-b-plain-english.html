No. 6A letter from the founderWhy this exists

A letter from the founder

From the founder

PMP · Lean Six Sigma Black Belt

Dear reader,

I have spent my working life in project management, and I believe in the discipline. A clear owner. A real date. One place to see where things stand.

What I do not believe in is making everyone learn the language first. Most project software has become needlessly confusing. It asks a wedding planner to think like a software team.

Signal Studio keeps the discipline and drops the jargon, the setup and the translating. We open in January 2027. I would like you to be there.

The founder Signal Studio
