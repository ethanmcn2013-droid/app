What we leave out

Clarity, not configuration.

  1. Onboarding wizard

    No setup.

    Start a project and write a line. You are already working.

  2. Methodology training

    No jargon.

    We write what is happening, who is doing it and when.

  3. Custom fields and rules

    Nothing to configure.

    No fields to build and no settings to tune before the work can start.

  4. Guest seats and logins

    No account to look.

    Send the timeline link. Your client, crew or class reads it without signing up.

A note from the founder

A note from the founder

PMP · Lean Six Sigma Black Belt

I have spent my working life in project management, and I believe in the discipline. A clear owner. A real date. One place to see where things stand.

What I don't believe in is making everyone learn the language first. Most project software has become needlessly confusing. It asks a wedding planner to think like a software team.

Signal Studio keeps the discipline and drops the jargon, the setup and the translating. We open in January 2027. I would like you to be there.
