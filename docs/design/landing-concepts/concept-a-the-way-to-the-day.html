What we left out

Project management without the project-manager energy.

Clarity, not configuration.

  1. 01

    No setup.

    Open it and write your first line. There is nothing to build before you start.

  2. 02

    No jargon.

    A job has a person and a day. You won't need to learn a new language to run your own work.

  3. 03

    Nothing to configure.

    No fields to design, no settings to get right first. It works the way you'd expect.

  4. 04

    No account to look.

    The people you share a timeline with just open the link. Family, clients, a whole class.

From the founder

Why I'm building this.

I'm a PMP and a Lean Six Sigma Black Belt. I've spent years inside project software, and I think most of it has become needlessly confusing.

Good project work has a real discipline, and it's worth keeping. The jargon, the setup and the translation layer are not. So I'm building something that keeps the first and drops the rest, for people who run real work and never wanted to become project managers.
