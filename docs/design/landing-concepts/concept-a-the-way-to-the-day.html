The way to the day

Sample

  2. TasksMon 28 Sep · on the board
  3. HomeTue 29 Sep · the next morning

Notes · Friday 25 September

It starts as a thought at the gate.

Aoife walks the grounds with Mara and Finn and types what they ask for as they go. The note is hers alone. When one line needs doing, she sends it on to Tasks and hands it to Orla. The rest stays private.

Private until you send a line on. Only that line travels.

Tasks · Monday 28 September

Now it's a job with a name and a day.

The line lands on the board as a card: Orla's, for Tuesday. No forms to fill in, nothing to set up. When she starts on it, it moves along, and the whole team can see where it is.

Board, list or calendar. The same jobs, however you like to look at them.

Home · Tuesday 29 September

In the morning, it's waiting for her.

Orla opens Signal Studio before work. Home tells her what needs her today, in plain words, and nothing else. She makes the call, the side room is held, and she ticks it off. A briefing, not a dashboard.

Timeline · Thursday 1 October

The families see it without asking.

Mara drops one link into the family group chat. Anyone can open it. No account, no app. The evening in the side room is on the timeline now, beside everything else that's settled.

Grandparents, cousins, the best man. Whoever taps the link sees the same page.

Saturday 22 May 2027. At six, the side room is ready. Nobody had to chase it, and nobody had to learn anything to see it.

Why I'm building this

From the founder
Today

The founderSignal Studio

I'm a PMP and a Lean Six Sigma Black Belt. I've spent years inside project software, and I think most of it has become needlessly confusing.

Good project work has a real discipline, and it's worth keeping. The jargon, the setup and the translation layer are not. So I'm building something that keeps the first and drops the rest, for people who run real work and never wanted to become project managers.
