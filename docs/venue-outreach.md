# Wedding venue partnership outreach · drafts

## What we're offering

- **Bulk wedding-tier codes** to hand to couples at booking time.
- $79 wedding tier, free to the venue, free to the couple.
- No markup, no logo placement, no contract.
- Premise: the couples who use Tasks remember the venue that
  pointed them at it.

## Drafts

One Gmail draft is already saved (The Villa, Virginia Beach —
`info@thevillava.com`). Four more are templated below. Look up the
real contact email at each venue's site, then either save as a
Gmail draft via the existing draft as a starting point, or copy +
paste into Gmail directly.

**Cadence:** No more than 5 venues per week. Personalized openers
matter — the line that names something specific about *their*
venue is the difference between a 5% reply rate and a 25% reply
rate.

## Tracking

Once any venue replies positively, log it here. Watch for:

- **Reply rate** — anything above 20% is unusually good. Below 5% means
  the opener line wasn't specific enough. Re-personalize the next batch.
- **Code redemption rate** — % of codes the venue actually hands out.
  If it's <30%, the venue isn't pitching it; consider a venue-facing
  one-pager that explains the value to the couple. If it's >70%, scale
  the program.
- **Couple-side conversion** — % of redeeming couples who upgrade
  beyond the wedding tier or refer another couple.
