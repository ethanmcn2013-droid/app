## The ballots that block you, worst first

### overall 7.3 · owned 7.4 · Revise

**Required.** Spend the byte surplus on height. Cut today at 390 from 3758px and the quiet and new-user mobile pages below one and a half screens by removing the restated provenance, the per-claim 'As read on' repetition and the persistent EDGE OF THIS READ block on worlds where nothing is past the line. Then either make the <details> actions perform their transition or rename them so they read as explanations, and restore the persistent rail the direction is named for or drop the claim.

Weakest: `rail__today__owner_signature__390x844.png — 3758px, the tallest page in the lab, 381px worse than the next`

Concerns:
- It is the tallest direction at both viewports and the worst offender exactly where height is least defensible. Today at 390 is 3758px; owner_quiet at 390 is 1534px and new_user at 390 is 1631px, roughly 300–800px taller than the same worlds elsewhere. On a phone, a day with nothing on it and an account with nothing in it both cost about two screens of scrolling. That is furniture, and the brief names empty furniture as rejected on sight.
- The <details> actions look like actions and are not. 'Mark as read', 'Snooze' and 'Clear from Inbox' render as indigo links; activating them opens an explanation rather than performing the transition. That is more honest than Desk's inert bold text and still leaves journeys 5 through 9 unbuilt, with 24 nested operable regions per Inbox to unpick when they are.
- The second column repeats rather than adds. On Analytics the left gutter prints 'As read on Thursday 16 July.' once per claim — three times in a single fold — and the right column restates provenance already in the body. On the Inbox it precomputes snooze return times (01:30 on Sunday 25 October, 02:00 on Sunday 28 March) for rows nobody has snoozed, which is furniture that will not survive a real implementation.
- The rendered pixels no longer match the direction's own thesis. There is no persistent 240px rail anywhere in these captures — navigation sits in a top header with the mode row under the headline — so the 'strongest persistent orientation for frequent mode-switching' claim is unevidenced by the only evidence available. The README's height table is stale on this direction too (it claims today = 1565px; measured 2621px), which means the written account of Rail cannot be relied on at all.

Second, suppress or visibly qualify any count on a mode whose read failed — a number and "DID NOT ANSWER" may never sit together. Directly above it the strip prints "Inbox 5" with "DID NOT ANSWER" underneath: a hard count presented as reliable, sitting against a claim that the mode returned nothing. ### overall 8.2 · owned 8.2 · Revise

**Required.** Two things. Remove the snooze-schedule panel from the Inbox arrival state — surface it when a snooze is actually being set, never before. And rank Today's three decisions visibly, so the cap reads as an editor's order rather than the top of a list.
