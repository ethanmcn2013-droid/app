## Passing ballots — read these to know what NOT to break

**overall 8.8 · owned 9** — protect:
- The banner ladder is named in words, in mono caps, across the full measure: nothing on a clean read, "COVERAGE · PARTLY READ" in amber, "FAILURE · PARTLY READ" in red between two rules, "NOT CONNECTED · NOTHING SET UP YET" in plain black. A broken provider and a quiet day are separated by a band that exists in one and not the other, and the band says which it is.
- The best new-reader sentence in the lab: "Nothing has been set up yet, so there was nothing to read. This is not a failed read, and it is not an all clear either." It names both wrong readings and refuses both, then gives a real action (Where to start, Open Tasks). This is Amendment 2 answered exactly.
- Analytics attaches a fixed ledger to every claim — COUNTED, CANNOT SAY, WINDOW, WHAT THE RECORDS SAY — so a caveat cannot be omitted by an author who forgets. "No accepted baseline" is printed as a value rather than left blank, and the time-zone caveat is stated on the overdue count where it actually bites. The Inbox count is honest about itself: "5 open, and at least one more that could not be counted."

**overall 8.6 · owned 8.9** — protect:
- "CANNOT SAY" as a named, permanent field on every Analytics claim is the best structural idea in the lab for truth language: it gives the limitation its own slot, so a claim shipped without a caveat is visibly incomplete rather than merely quiet.
- "5 open, and at least one more that could not be counted." is the single best sentence I read anywhere in this evidence. It refuses to state a total, states why, and sits in the margin next to the list it governs.
- The new-user screen is the most precisely worded of the four: "Nothing has been set up yet, so there was nothing to read. This is not a failed read, and it is not an all clear either", supported by "There is no project yet. Nothing failed, and nothing here counts as zero."

**overall 8.7 · owned 8.6** — protect:
- The only direction that has actually built the interactive half of the product and paid for it in the open. Its Inbox renders 21 real <button type="button"> controls with aria-expanded, backed by one client module: 13.9 KB of code after comments, 3.4 KB gzipped at source, importing exactly two things — react, and a type-only import from the shell that erases at build. It is route-local, so it never touches the 246.1/247 KB shared runtime, sits comfortably inside the 41.2 KB total_client_js headroom, and forms its own small chunk rather than pressing on largest_chunk at 62.5/63.
- It writes down the boundary that hard constraint 2 requires and nobody else acknowledges: an explicit MAY IMPORT / MAY NOT list naming useTaskPanel, useTasks, useDomain, useColumnConfig, usePersonalization and useCalendarFrame as forbidden directly or transitively. That is the single most valuable engineering artefact in this lab, because it is the drift that would make Home inherit the Tasks runtime and the /welcome redirect.
- State is expressed as shape plus colour and the shape changes across worlds: a filled amber square, a hollow square for not-connected, a red target for failure, and a full-width banner rule labelled FAILURE · PARTLY READ, COVERAGE · PARTLY READ or NOT CONNECTED · NOTHING SET UP YET. Every mode carries its own state in the mode row, so the health of a mode you are not in is legible from the one you are in, and it degrades correctly with colour removed.

**overall 9 · owned 9.1** — protect:
- A real three-axis grid that holds for the entire page height: a right-aligned marginal column carrying the dateline and running annotations, the prose measure, and a right-aligned figures column of lateness and dates set in mono. Everything hangs off three vertical edges and one shared right edge. This is the only direction where the full width is genuinely a composition rather than a leftover, and the figures column is the classic editorial ledger done properly.
- The failure state is a masthead slug: two red rules bracketing FAILURE · PARTLY READ in mono, above the headline. It is unmistakable at 1440 and it survives intact at 390. Paired with the three-state markers (hollow square not connected, amber square partly read, red target failure) and a matching slug-rule colour, a quiet day, a partial read, a first-ever screen and a broken provider are four visibly different pages.
- Analytics turns the left margin into a labelled field key (COUNTED, CANNOT SAY, WINDOW) with the values in the measure and the numeral set small and right-aligned at the page edge. Evidence rendered as a set of fields with no container at all, and it is the most confident single screen in the lab.
