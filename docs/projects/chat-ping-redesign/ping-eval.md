# Ping test script

The fixed script every "Ping feels intelligent" review runs, so scores compare between passes. Do not change it between passes without the founder's agreement.

How to run: reset the prototype (Prototype menu, Reset) before each section. "In Niamh" means open the Niamh Kelly chat and type into its composer. "In Ping" means open the Ping chat. Type the request exactly as written and press Enter. Where a line follows another, do them in order without resetting.

Mark each line **pass**, **weak** or **fail**:

- **pass**: Ping understood, did the right thing (or asked one sensible question), and pointed at its source where it used one. A busy venue manager would trust it.
- **weak**: roughly right, but generic, slightly off (wrong owner, missing date, vague wording), or it needed the scripted phrasing.
- **fail**: misunderstood, guessed wrongly, made something up, or gave the generic "I can..." fallback when it should have handled it.

## A. Plain requests (in Ping)

1. `What's late this week?`
   Good: names the tonic and olives order as late (Dara, 2 days), and says what's due today.
2. `Catch me up on unread`
   Good: groups what needs a decision (marquee sides, Ciarán's request) and what's just good to know, each with a link to the message.
3. `Create tasks for the tasting on 1 August`
   Good: a plan of a few tasks with owners and dates, grounded in Dara's message about the tasting.

## B. Free-form requests (in Ping)

4. `remind me to chase the couple for the dietary list on monday`
   Good: one task, "Chase Mara and Finn for the dietary list", owner you, due Mon 20 Jul. Notices the existing dietary-list task rather than making a blind duplicate.
5. `get aoife to order more prosecco for saturday`
   Good: a task owned by Aoife, due Sat 18 Jul. Ideally notes she chose spritz and ordered 6 cases.
6. `what does dara need from me?`
   Good: the final dietary list before he locks the service notes, quoting his message, with a link.
7. `when is the floor walk?`
   Good: Friday 24 July with Niamh, who's bringing the new seating plan. Quotes the source.
8. `move the run-sheet to tomorrow`
   Good: a plan changing the run-sheet task's due date from Today to Tomorrow, shown before anything changes.
9. `what's on for saturday?`
   Good: the wedding (ceremony 2pm), marquee sides still to confirm, rain after 3pm, mains at 7:20. Grounded, not invented.

## C. Vague or ambiguous (in Ping)

10. `can you sort the marquee thing`
    Good: works out it's the open marquee-sides decision, and asks one short question (for example "Confirm full sides with Byrne Events, or make it a task for Aoife?") with tap-to-answer options.
11. `make a task for the bar`
    Good: asks one short question about what the task is, offering options from the chats (for example moving the bar to the orangery), rather than creating "For the bar".
12. `give it to niamh`  (straight after 11, with no task yet made)
    Good: asks what "it" is, or uses the task under discussion. Never invents one.

## D. Inside a conversation (in Niamh)

13. `@Ping turn this into a task`
    Good: "Decide on the marquee sides" due today, quoting "call it today" and linking to Niamh's 08:52 message.
14. Apply the plan from 13.
    Good: the created task appears as a card, with one sensible next step offered (for example "Tell Niamh you're on it?").
15. `@Ping turn this into a task`  (again, straight after 14)
    Good: notices it already made that task and says so, offering to open it instead of duplicating.
16. `@Ping who's carrying the bar?`
    Good: two of the floor team at 1pm, per Niamh in the thread, with a link to the thread.
17. Send `can you ring Byrne Events at 11?`, wait for Niamh's reply "I'll ring Byrne Events at 11...", then `@Ping make that a task`
    Good: owner Niamh, due Today 11:00, quoting her reply.

## E. Remembering within the session (in Ping)

18. `Create tasks for the tasting on 1 August`, then press Cancel, then ask again: `Create tasks for the tasting on 1 August`
    Good: remembers the earlier cancelled plan and says so ("You cancelled this earlier. Here it is again" or asks what to change).
19. After applying a task in D, ask in Ping: `what have you done for me today?`
    Good: lists what it created or changed this session, with links.

## F. Honest limits (in Ping)

20. `send niamh a message saying thanks`
    Good: says plainly it can't send messages for you yet, and offers the nearest useful thing (open the Niamh chat with the message drafted, or similar).
21. `book a table at the local restaurant for friday`
    Good: says it can't do that, and what it would need. No made-up action.
22. `what's the weather on saturday?`
    Good: doesn't pretend to look up the weather. Can point to Niamh's Met Éireann note (rain likely after 3pm), clearly labelled as hers.

## G. Quiet, well-timed help

23. Open Niamh's chat and wait 2 seconds without typing.
    Good: at most one quiet suggestion, at the right message, easy to dismiss. Not shown again once dismissed.
24. Open the couple's chat (Mara and Finn). Finn asks "Can we send the full list Monday?"
    Good: if Ping offers anything, it's useful (for example noting Dara needs the list before he locks the service notes). Nothing noisy.
25. Use "Catch up" in the run-sheet task thread.
    Good: a short summary of what's decided (speeches before mains, mains at 7:20) and what's still open on the checklist, with links.

## Scoring note

Reviewers report the count of pass, weak and fail, and quote Ping's actual reply for every weak or fail line.
