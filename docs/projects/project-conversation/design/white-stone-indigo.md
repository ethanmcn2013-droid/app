# Project conversation visual direction

12 September 2026 · approved prototype override

The Project Conversation prototype uses a crisp white working field, stone-black application rail and indigo for active state, focus and directed attention. Grey and cream do not act as page or panel surfaces. Muted stone text and fine stone dividers may clarify hierarchy without turning the workspace papery.

The desktop structure is a compact dark app rail, a white grouped conversation index, a broad white author-row feed and an optional white related-work rail. The conversation title and audience lead the feed, followed by a compact view row. The composer is a substantial white framed control anchored below the message history. Message bodies remain open rows rather than chat bubbles.

On narrow screens, the app rail moves to the bottom, the conversation index becomes an icon strip, related work stays available through the header control, and the audience remains readable. Synthetic and preview-only truth remains visible in every layout.

This direction borrows structure and density from the supplied conversation references. It does not borrow their branding, dark feed, AI actions, decorative gradients or broad feature set. The prototype's consent, access, receipt recovery, private Notes and explicit Tasks handoff behavior remains authoritative.
