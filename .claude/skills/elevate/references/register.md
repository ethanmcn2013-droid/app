# The register (default brief)

The default aesthetic north for an engagement that does not bring its own:
the monochrome instrument school — the register running through the xAI/
Grok, X, and SpaceX surfaces. Whether one designer made all of them is
irrelevant and is never claimed; what matters is that the register is
coherent and encodable. This file encodes its PRINCIPLES. It never copies
any brand's trade dress, marks, or assets — a skill run must not produce
work that could be mistaken for those companies' property.

A brief can replace this file's palette and materials wholesale (a client
with a warm editorial brand should not receive an instrument panel). The
principles section still applies to any register: they are about
discipline, not darkness.

## The principles

1. **Monochrome severity.** The ground is a true value — near-black or
   true white — never a hedge of mid-greys. Grey exists only as tints of
   the ink over the ground, on a declared alpha ladder. If a neutral is
   used it was chosen, with a stated hue bias, not inherited.
2. **One accent, spent like a laser.** A single accent colour, used so
   rarely that its every appearance is information. The accent never
   decorates; it points. If the accent appears more than a handful of
   times in a viewport, it has become wallpaper and the register is gone.
3. **Telemetry typography.** Numerals are the heroes: large, tabular,
   engineered-looking. Micro-labels are uppercase, letterspaced, small,
   and mono. Hierarchy is carried by size, weight and density of ink —
   never by colour. Two weights maximum. Every line-height declared.
4. **Hairline structure.** Rules and borders at 1px and low alpha do the
   work cards and boxes do in lesser systems. Shadows are reserved for
   objects that genuinely float, and there are few of those.
5. **Data is the decoration.** Charts, gauges, readouts and frames of the
   real product are the only imagery. No stock, no illustration-for-mood,
   no gradient heroes. If a surface has no data to show, it earns beauty
   through type and space.
6. **Motion is physics, not personality.** Instant response, short settle,
   one easing family. Nothing bounces. Nothing floats idly. Motion exists
   to confirm cause and effect, and reduced-motion is honoured completely.
7. **Deletion as craft.** Every element must survive the question "what
   breaks if this goes?" Chrome that merely frames gets deleted. The
   register's luxury is space, and space is bought with deletion.
8. **Severity never becomes illegibility.** Contrast passes AA against the
   REAL composited ground at every size. The instrument look fails the
   moment an operator squints.
9. **The words stay warm.** The severity is visual. Copy remains plain,
   human, and confident — the register's voice is a calm engineer, not a
   cold machine. No jargon, no exclamation marks, sentence case.

## The default materials (Signal Studio)

- **Ink** `#111111` — the ground when dark, the text when light, and the
  tint source for every line, wash and secondary tone (declared alphas:
  0.72 / 0.62 / 0.28 / 0.16 / 0.08 / 0.06 / 0.04 are the proven ladder).
- **White** `#ffffff` — the paper. Sheets of it lifted off an ink floor is
  the house composition.
- **Indigo** `#4f46e5` — the one accent that school does not have: the
  moment. On dark grounds it lifts to `#6f68ee` for contrast.
- **Geist** 400/600 and **Geist Mono** 400 — nothing else, no other
  weights.
- **Status by ink density and fill, never hue.** Filled means urgent,
  outlined means current, the accent means the one next thing. This single
  rule is what lets a three-colour palette run a working product.

## How the panel holds the register

The Brand seat judges whether the palette is being SPENT well or merely
obeyed — obedience is the gate's job. The Taste seat asks whether a
discerning stranger would believe a top studio shipped it. The measured
gate enforces the materials mechanically so neither seat wastes a finding
on a stray hex.
